# The Cartographers of Nowhere

[Civilization gallery](../../docs/Civilizations.md)

![Grand Surveyor Elian Vey in the diplomacy scene](../../docs/artwork/leaders/TheCartographersOfNowhere.jpg)

Grand Surveyor Elian Vey leads this human-only civilization from Meridian. Meridian's surveyors record what exists beyond the map's edge. Elian Vey keeps a space on every map for discoveries yet to come.

- **The Unfinished Atlas**. Every ten previously unseen land plots discovered by your units give 15 Science and 10 Culture at Standard speed, ten rewards per era. Unspent progress carries forward. City-only discoveries are unavailable because their source cannot be verified.
- **Pathfinder** replaces Scout. +1 Sight and ignores terrain movement costs subject to ordinary movement restrictions. Unique bonuses persist through upgrades.
- **Atlas House** replaces Library. +1 Science. +1 Culture after revealing 100 land plots and +1 Gold after revealing 200, from any source.

The replacements inherit the active standalone Community Patch v151 definitions and approved companion effects. The pack includes original emblems, flags, portraits, diplomacy scenes, Dawn illustrations and setup maps. The replacement units and buildings inherit their in-world models. [Art and compilation notes](../docs/DailyExpansion_021_035_Art.md)

TileRevealed carries explicit unit provenance only. The ability credits verified in-session own-unit discoveries. City-only and pre-context starting reveals cannot be distinguished from external reveals and are excluded.
