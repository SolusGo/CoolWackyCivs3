# The Chachapoya

Guardian Yana leads this human-only civilization from Kuélap. Inspired by the Chachapoya peoples of the cloud forests in northern Peru and their stone settlements. Guardian Yana is a fictional representative leader of these varied communities.

- **Cities Above the Clouds**. +1 Culture in cities founded on Hills. Independently, +1 Science in cities with a Walls-class building.
- **Cloud Sentinel** replaces Composite Bowman. +1 Sight and +15% defense on Hills. No bonus to ranged attacks. Unique properties persist on upgrades.
- **Cloud Terrace** replaces Granary. +1 Food. +1 Production in Hill-founded cities.

The replacements inherit the active standalone Community Patch v151 definitions and approved companion effects. The civilization emblem and flag are original geometric artwork. Leader, Dawn, map, diplomacy and replacement portraits use stock fallback art.

The Cloud Sentinel's melee-only modifier is scoped around native resolution, so pre-battle previews may omit it.
