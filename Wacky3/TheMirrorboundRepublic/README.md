# The Mirrorbound Republic

First Reflector Caelis leads this human-only civilization from Verity. In Verity, failure is studied even when it is painful to remember. Its philosophers record mistakes in glass so reforms can be compared with what came before.

- **Lessons Written in Glass**. Eligible military deaths against foreign major military units grant 1 Lesson, up to 5. Spend 2 for +10% combat for 3 owner-turns or 3 for +10% Capital Science for 5. Each has a 10-owner-turn cooldown.
- **Reflection Guard** replaces Swordsman. +1 Sight. After surviving hostile melee combat on defense, gains +15% strength on its next attack, expiring by the end of its next owner-turn.
- **Chamber of Revision** replaces University. +1 Culture and +1 Science, plus +1 Science with at least 3 stored Lessons.

The replacements inherit the active standalone Community Patch v151 definitions and approved companion effects. The civilization emblem and flag are original geometric artwork. Leader, Dawn, map, diplomacy and replacement portraits use stock fallback art.

Direct combat, verified interceptions and scoped ranged collateral are covered. Native dispatch and attack consumption still require acceptance.
