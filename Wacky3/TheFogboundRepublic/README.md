# The Fogbound Republic

[Civilization gallery](../../docs/Civilizations.md)

![First Warden Mirelle in the diplomacy scene](../../docs/artwork/leaders/TheFogboundRepublic.jpg)

First Warden Mirelle leads this human-only civilization from Greyfen. Greyfen's watchtowers and raised paths thread through the mist. Mirelle guards their hidden approaches and studies the wetland ecology.

- **The Living Mist**. +1 Science per worked, unpillaged Marsh, capped at +4 per city. +1 Culture with two owned Marsh plots within three hexes. Military units gain +10% defense on Marsh.
- **Fen Stalker** replaces Scout. +1 Sight, double Marsh movement and +15% additional defense on Marsh. Unique properties persist through upgrades.
- **Marsh Conservatory** replaces Library. +1 Science. At least two owned Marsh plots within three hexes give +1 Food; at least four give another +1 Science.

The replacements inherit the active standalone Community Patch v151 definitions and approved companion effects. The pack includes original emblems, flags, portraits, diplomacy scenes, Dawn illustrations and setup maps. The replacement units and buildings inherit their in-world models. [Art and compilation notes](../docs/DailyExpansion_021_035_Art.md)

Native timing, combat and save acceptance remain outstanding.
