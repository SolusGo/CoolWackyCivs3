# Kingdom of Makuria strategy

Develop protected cities and build Walls when the combined defensive and +1 Culture/+1 Faith value fits the opening economy. Use extra Bowman Sight to choose defensive lines; its +15% applies only while defending, including defense against ranged attacks.

Painted Churches add ordinary Temple utility plus +1 Culture/+1 Science. Midgame religious and cultural choices still use native systems. Each walled Church city adds +2 Culture/+1 Faith/+1 Science in unique base yields; it still needs population, workers and other science infrastructure.

For the late cultural game, build Tourism and pursue appropriate religious and policy options. Counterplay targets exposed expansion and forces Bowmen into offensive exchanges. The bonuses are flat across speeds, local to each qualifying city and removed when its last Walls-class building disappears.

See the [complete civilization guide](../../TheKingdomOfMakuria/README.md) for exact mechanics, unit/building inheritance, ownership policy, native testing requirements and limitations. Automated evidence is not a native gameplay pass.
