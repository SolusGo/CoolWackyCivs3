# Playing the Candlekeeper's Covenant

Seraphine favors Culture or Science with a defensive army. Build Halls in every city, protect the Capital, and develop normal science/culture infrastructure while the world's lost cities gradually strengthen your archives. You do not need to meet a civilization, see its city or fight its wars to remember its destruction.

Each Lost Flame adds one recurring capital Culture; three Flames add one capital Science. Halls always add one Culture and gain one Science at three Flames, two at nine. Nine Flames and six Halls give +15 Culture and +15 Science across the empire before normal modifiers, in addition to inherited Temple effects. Flames cap at twelve and survive capital loss.

Captures supply no Flames. Burning your own Covenant-owned cities or gifting Covenant-founded cities for foreign destruction supplies none. The last foreign major owner matters for ordinary cities. Treat the ability as patient support, not a reason to turn your economy into a conquest machine.

Vigil Keepers retain active Pikeman statistics, gain one Sight and receive 15% defense only in native friendly territory. Their Sight and lineage survive upgrades. Use them to watch and hold approaches to your archives. Native qualifying Open Borders also counts as friendly, but neutral and enemy ground does not; the promotion never boosts attacks.

Yield changes detected at the post-economy player-turn boundary affect a subsequent payout. Native timing and combat acceptance are outstanding; consult the civilization guide and testing report for observable script limitations.
