# The Salt Road Compact strategy

Open safe land routes and spread origins when route profitability and security permit. A city with five outgoing routes gains the same UA as one with one route. Exchanges add permanent Food/Gold and conditional Science; incoming and sea routes activate neither conditional controller.

In the midgame, protect corridors with upgraded Escorts and place fights on favorable terrain. An attack into Desert qualifies even when launched from Grassland; attacking from Desert into Grassland does not. Desert defense includes Hills and feature tiles.

Convert Production and commerce into late research infrastructure and diplomatic resources. Route capacity and diplomatic votes remain native. Plunder, sea-oriented maps and cancellation can interrupt the economy. A recalled route is excluded at the next reconciliation; expired routes lose conditional yields while Exchange Food/Gold remain. Flat yield amounts do not scale with game speed.

See the [complete civilization guide](../../TheSaltRoadCompact/README.md) for exact mechanics, unit/building inheritance, ownership policy, native testing requirements and limitations. Automated evidence is not a native gameplay pass.
