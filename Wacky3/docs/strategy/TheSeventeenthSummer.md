# Playing The Seventeenth Summer

Grow during Dormancy and prepare queues before Emergence. At Standard speed, Ixa spends thirteen owner-turns gathering strength, then four in synchronized creation. Brood Nurseries preserve normal Granary benefits and add Faith throughout the cycle; during Emergence they bring each city's unique bonuses to +3 Production and +1 Culture, alongside the permanent +1 Faith.

Favor Science and Culture victories: invest the short Production bursts in infrastructure, wonders and cultural buildings while steady population growth supports research. Preserve early Shellguards as veterans. Their lineage survives multiple upgrades and reawakens each Emergence; their extra Sight belongs to the original Shellguard type. Ordinary Spearmen cannot become brood veterans through the normal shared upgrade path.

Plan military campaigns around the +20% attack/defense window, and remember its end. At processed Standard owner-turn 14 combat changes immediately, while the phase's city yields are scheduled for the next economic collection. The same next-payout rule applies when Dormancy returns on 18. Both city rates and combat use the displayed phase between callbacks. Notifications describe this explicitly.

Quick cycles are 9/3, Epic 20/6 and Marathon 39/12. Wars and eras never restart the clock. A new city joins the owner's current phase immediately, and a captured Nursery only supplies conditional Production under eligible Summer ownership. See the [civilization guide](../../TheSeventeenthSummer/README.md) for exact timing and unique totals.
