# Winning with the Ministry of Almost

Your objective is a mature economy that wins after the emergency program ends. Think of the UA as temporary support for a normal Science or Culture plan.

## 1. An ordinary Ancient Era civilization

At a gap of zero through two you receive no UA. Scout, settle good land, improve food and production, protect workers and establish research infrastructure. The Academy retains the active CP Library's normal effects, so prioritize it on the same economic grounds as an ordinary Library. Its permanent Culture helps policies throughout the game.

The Second Wind Guard's extra Movement helps cover several cities and reach a defensive tile sooner. Its strength is still the CP Spearman's strength. Friendly team-owned terrain gives +15% defense; attacking receives no extra modifier. Combine terrain, ranged support and sensible roads rather than treating Guards as an assault army.

## 2. A technologically disadvantaged challenger

Three to five technologies behind the leading living rival major team grants one Science and one Culture per city. Four cities therefore gain **4 Science and 4 Culture per turn from the UA**. If all four also own Academies, their permanent Culture makes the total unique contribution **4 Science and 8 Culture** beyond normal Libraries.

Use that support to complete essential infrastructure and keep growing. Meet rivals and trade normally; the gap includes unmet eligible teams too. An eliminated leader stops counting at the next owner snapshot. Your own teammates are excluded, and their research is already part of your team's count.

## 3. Temporary emergency support

At six or more technologies behind, each city gains exactly two Science and one Culture. Every Academy additionally gains one Production. One city at gap seven has unique base bonuses **2 Science + 2 Culture + 1 Production**. Four cities with four Academies at gap six produce **8 Science + 8 Culture + 4 Production**. Gap ten pays exactly the same as gap six.

Spend the window building enduring capacity: population, improved land, Universities, science specialists when sustainable, later research buildings, and the production to finish them. Maintain enough defense to protect those investments. A captured city receives the current tier without retroactive awards and obeys ordinary resistance rules. Growing an empire can help, but bad cities still have expansion costs and infrastructure needs.

Do not postpone key research to enlarge or preserve the gap. The lost unlocks, military safety and accumulated Science usually matter much more than these small capped flat yields. There is no permanent stacking reward for remaining behind.

## 4. Compete without the UA

When the next snapshot falls from six to five, each of four Academy cities loses one Science and one Production: empire totals change from **8/8/4** to **4/8/0** in Science/Culture/Production. Falling from three to two removes the UA entirely; the four Academies retain only their **4 permanent Culture** beyond normal Libraries. Do not build a budget that depends on the temporary Production continuing.

For Science, sustain growth and specialists, reach later research infrastructure on time and develop enough production for the victory finish. For Culture, convert policy support and Academy development into Great Works, thematic collections and actual tourism. Continue normal diplomacy, trade and defense. The UA helps you approach the leaders; your permanent economy must finish the job.
