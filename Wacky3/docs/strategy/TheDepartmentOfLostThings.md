# Department of Lost Things strategy

Use a compact, defensible opening with Officers scouting approaches. Recover Curios through necessary nearby combat; prioritize incomplete city collections rather than chasing distant kills. Offices amplify established collections without changing the four-Curio cap.

In the midgame, protect the economic core and improve ordinary research and population. At four Curios an Office city's unique recurring total is +8 Culture/+4 Science/+4 Gold. This is a fixed city bonus, so research multipliers, specialists and ordinary development become more important as the game advances.

Convert the late economy into Science infrastructure or Tourism. Culture is not Tourism, and capped cities gain nothing unique from further fighting. Opponents can deny recovery by fighting beyond radius three or holding the city; capture pauses its collection for the original Department player. Reload and recapture restore only the same surviving city lifetime.
