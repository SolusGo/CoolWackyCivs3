# Assembly of Scarecrows strategy

Plan six Farms near each settlement, with overlap where terrain allows. Three Farms unlock Culture/Production; six unlock Food. Farms need not be worked, so use strong ordinary tiles and specialists as appropriate. Bells add permanent Culture and capped Gold at three/six/nine Farms.

In the midgame, protect threshold Farms with Sentinels and repair pillaging promptly. More than nine Farms add no further unique Gold. Maintain happiness and normal research while Food supports growth and specialist population. Native friendly battle tiles, including qualifying Open Borders, determine Sentinel strength.

Turn population and policies into cultural buildings, specialists and Tourism in the late game. Poor agricultural maps and improvement competition are meaningful weaknesses. Opponents should pillage decisive Farms and force combat outside native friendly territory. No game-speed scaling changes these thresholds.
