# Playing the Hollow Choir

Look for a productive Hill city center beside one or two Mountains. The Hill supplies one Production, while adjacent Mountains independently supply up to two Faith even before you own them. A strong flatland site still earns the Faith; avoid sacrificing food, resources and defensibility merely to collect a small terrain bonus.

Build ordinary economic infrastructure and Resonance Halls as Amphitheaters become available. Halls retain the normal Amphitheater and add one Culture. Acquire two Mountains within three hexes for one Science, or four for the two-Science cap. Shared Mountains can serve several Halls. Mountains cannot be worked by this civilization, and buying more than four for one Hall gives no additional Science. Keep enough productive land to grow and build.

Use Chorus Sentinels to watch approaches and defend Hills. Their extra Sight and hill defense persist on upgrades, making veterans useful through later eras. The unique promotion adds no attacking strength; fight on favorable defensive ground while maintaining the normal Swordsman resource and technology requirements.

Culture and Faith support development while the small Science bonus rewards suitable territory. Continue the normal Science infrastructure for a Science victory, or build Great Works and tourism for Culture. A terrain-poor city receives no compensation. Losing Mountain tiles or a Hall reduces Science, so protect borders and rebuild damaged infrastructure.
