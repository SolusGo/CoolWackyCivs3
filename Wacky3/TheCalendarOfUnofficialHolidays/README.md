# The Calendar of Unofficial Holidays

Minister Pippa Day leads this human-only civilization from Longweekend. Pippa Day's parliament resolves municipal disagreements by declaring commemorative days. Workshops and markets put the celebrations to use.

- **A Reason to Celebrate**. +2 Production and +1 Science in a city while its We Love the King Day counter is active. Does not trigger or extend celebrations.
- **Parade Marshal** replaces Pikeman. +1 Sight and +15% defense in friendly territory. Both persist through normal upgrades.
- **Holiday Office** replaces Colosseum. +1 Culture and +2 Gold while We Love the King Day is active. Retains the normal Colosseum effects.

The replacements inherit the active standalone Community Patch v151 definitions and approved companion effects. The civilization emblem and flag are original geometric artwork. Leader, Dawn, map, diplomacy and replacement portraits use stock fallback art.

Native production uses the counter before the city countdown step. Treasury and research use the state later in the same turn. One-turn boundary behavior requires native measurement.
