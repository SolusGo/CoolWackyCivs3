# The Republic of Borrowed Tomorrows

[Civilization gallery](../../docs/Civilizations.md)

![Chancellor Aevra Voss in the diplomacy scene](../../docs/artwork/leaders/TheRepublicOfBorrowedTomorrows.jpg)

Chancellor Aevra Voss leads this human-only civilization from Tomorrow's End. The chancellor negotiates with future generations as though they were creditors seated in the room. The Temporal Exchange records every promise.

- **The Future Is Collateral**. Production and research advances are unavailable in this build. The engine has no verified settlement hook that can withhold and reconcile the exact debt after completion and overflow.
- **Debt Collector** replaces Pikeman. +15% Combat Strength in friendly territory. The additional +10% during repayment requires the blocked advance mechanic.
- **Temporal Exchange** replaces Market. +1 Gold. +1 Science while debt free. The alternate +1 Production requires the blocked advance mechanic.

The replacements inherit the active standalone Community Patch v151 definitions and approved companion effects. The pack includes original emblems, flags, portraits, diplomacy scenes, Dawn illustrations and setup maps. The replacement units and buildings inherit their in-world models. [Art and compilation notes](../docs/DailyExpansion_021_035_Art.md)

Exact debt-neutral settlement is blocked. Only the active Pikeman/Market replacements, +15% friendly strength and debt-free Exchange yields are enabled.
