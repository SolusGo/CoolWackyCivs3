# The Glass Orchard

First Gardener Solenne leads this human-only civilization from Sunspire. Solenne's glasshouses shade orchards from the desert heat while gathering light. Glasswardens protect the workers and the carefully tended soil.

- **Harvest the Sun**. +1 Food per worked, unpillaged Desert, capped at +4. Desert-founded cities gain +1 Science. While at peace with every living major, working three Desert plots adds +2 Gold.
- **Glasswarden** replaces Spearman. +1 Sight and +15% defense on Desert. Unique bonuses persist through upgrades.
- **Solar Conservatory** replaces Garden. +1 Food and +1 Science per two worked Desert tiles, capped at +2 Science. +1 Culture if founded on Desert. No freshwater requirement.

The replacements inherit the active standalone Community Patch v151 definitions and approved companion effects. The civilization emblem and flag are original geometric artwork. Leader, Dawn, map, diplomacy and replacement portraits use stock fallback art.

Native timing, combat and save acceptance remain outstanding.
